Help my co-founder and me plan the launch of a food company, and write it up as a launch plan document.

The business
- We make a tofu-based "cream cheese" spread in three flavors: plain, chive and smoked chili.
- We are based in Oakland, California, and currently make small batches in a home kitchen.
- The product must be kept refrigerated. Our test batches last about 14 days.

Goals
- Selling at local farmers markets within 6 months.
- On the shelf at 20 independent grocers in the Bay Area within 18 months.
- Carried by a major grocery chain within 5 years.

Constraints
- We have $40,000 in savings and do not want outside investment in the first 18 months.
- I work on this full time. My co-founder keeps a day job and has evenings and weekends only.
- The product contains soy and is made near tree nuts. Labeling must be correct from the first sale.
- We want to call it "cream cheese" on the label if we legally can. If we can't, propose alternatives.

What we need
- The licenses, permits and registrations required at each stage, in the order we need them.
- Whether to rent a commercial kitchen or use a co-packer, and when to switch.
- A price per tub, checked against three competing products and our costs.
- A month-by-month budget for the first 18 months that stays within our savings.
- A marketing plan for the farmers market stage.
- Five brand name ideas and a short tagline for each.

Make the decisions for us, but do not file, register or contact anyone. List every assumption you make and every decision you want us to confirm.
