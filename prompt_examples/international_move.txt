Help my wife and me plan a move from Chicago to Lisbon, Portugal, and write it up as a relocation plan document.

- Our budget for the move itself is $25,000, not counting housing in Lisbon.

- My wife takes a daily prescription medication and cannot have a gap in supply.
- Neither of us speaks Portuguese yet.

What we need
- Which visa each of us should apply for, what it requires, and a timeline working back from September 1.
- What my wife needs to do to have her nursing license recognized.
- The rules and timeline for bringing the dog, including the flight.
- How we get healthcare and her prescription from the first week.
- A comparison of selling and renting out the Chicago home, with the main tax points to raise with an accountant.
- Three neighborhoods that fit our constraints, with what daily life is like in each.
- A plan for learning Portuguese before we arrive.
- A short list of what to ship, sell or store.

Make the decisions for us, but do not apply, book or contact anyone. List every assumption you make and every decision you want us to confirm.
